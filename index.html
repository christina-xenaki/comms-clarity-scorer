          What it checks

          Checks: concreteness, strengths, empty-quote detection, buzzword density, sentence length, readability, passive voice, hedging, superlatives & absolute claims, nominalisation, self-reference, acronym load, inverted pyramid structure, consistency & typos, English variant, and regulated claims (off by default, and never counted towards the overall grade). See SCORING.md for exactly how each is calculated, including known limitations.

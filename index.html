    "Suggested rewrite" is an automatic fix — check the grammar before using it. "Worth asking yourself" is a question the tool can't answer for you.

      The analysis runs in your browser, on your device. Your text is never uploaded anywhere, so you can safely paste a draft that hasn't been published yet.

      This tool is directional, not authoritative — it does not replace an editor, and regulated-claim flags are a prompt to consult your legal/compliance/medical-affairs team, not a sign-off. A good score does not mean the release is accurate, newsworthy, or that it will earn coverage.

      How scoring works

      What it checks

      Checks: concreteness, strengths, empty-quote detection, buzzword density, sentence length, readability, passive voice, hedging, superlatives & absolute claims, nominalisation, self-reference, acronym load, inverted pyramid structure, consistency & typos, English variant, and regulated claims (off by default, and never counted toward the overall grade). See SCORING.md for exactly how each is calculated, including known limitations.
